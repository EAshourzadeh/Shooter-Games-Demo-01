Shooter Games

Two classroom games from the Adobe Animate days, rebuilt in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Open one and click or tap to shoot.

🐰 9 16 3 20

Number Shooter

The board shows a number word. Shoot the balloon with the matching number.

  • 9 stars, one number word each
  • 5 lives and 2 minutes per star
  • Balloons speed up as time runs out
Play
🚁 c h r p

Letter Shooter

A picture and a word with a gap. Shoot the missing letters, and never the same letter twice.

  • 12 picture words
  • 1 or 2 missing letters per word
  • A repeat shot or a wrong letter costs a life
Play

Presenting

Press F for full screen. Both games scale to any screen or projector.

Controls

Click or tap to shoot. M mutes sound, Esc returns to the menu.

Built with

No framework and no build step. One shared engine, one small file per game.
